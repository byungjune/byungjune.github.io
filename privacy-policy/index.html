Information We Collect

Our apps do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers. All user data (settings, preferences, history) is stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences and is never sent externally.

Third-Party Services

Our apps use the following third-party services that may collect certain information:

  • Google AdMob — Displays advertisements. AdMob may collect device information, advertising identifiers, and ad interaction data. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Collects anonymous usage statistics such as app opens, screen views, and crash reports. No personally identifiable information is collected. Firebase Privacy

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate any servers that store user data. If you uninstall the app, all local data is deleted.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

당사 앱은 다음과 같은 제3자 서비스를 사용하며, 이들 서비스가 일부 정보를 수집할 수 있습니다:
