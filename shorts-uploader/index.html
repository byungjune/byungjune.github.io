Why It Stands Uploader

A private publishing tool for the owner's own YouTube channels.

Why It Stands Uploader is a personal command-line tool. It uploads short documentary videos that the owner produced to the owner's own YouTube channels, sets their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, posts the channel's own first comment, and reads view statistics for those channels. It is not offered to other users.
