Privacy Policy

Shorts Channel Uploader · Last updated: September 27, 2026

Who uses this app

This app is used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not published for other people.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube Data API: uploads the owner's videos, updates their metadata and thumbnails, posts and moderates comments on the owner's own videos, and reads channel and video details.
  • YouTube Analytics API: reads view, watch-time and subscriber statistics for the owner's channels.

How the data is stored

OAuth tokens and fetched statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app does not collect data about viewers beyond the aggregated statistics YouTube provides to channel owners, and public comments it reads are used only to reply on the owner's channel.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
